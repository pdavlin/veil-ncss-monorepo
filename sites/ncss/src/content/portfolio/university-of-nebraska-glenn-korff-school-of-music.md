NCSS was contracted to deliver manual and motorized roller shades at UNL’s stunning new 103,000 SF performing arts center. Numerous details were coordinated throughout the submittal process before installation, which included multiple discussions balancing fabric acoustics, durability, and performance.

The PVC-free Mermet Greenscreen fabric was used throughout the facility, requiring NCSS to work closely with manufacturing partner Draper to ensure this long-lead fabric was available for the project’s multiple phases.
