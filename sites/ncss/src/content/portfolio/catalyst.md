Catalyst represents a true commitment to the concept of adaptive reuse. The Omaha Steel Works building - originally built in 1906 - went through a multi-year complete renovation to be reborn as an extraordinary co-working and incubation space adjacent to the University of Nebraska Medicine campus in Omaha, NE.

NCSS was contracted to deliver a carefully coordinated Draper motorized and manual roller shade system in the primary build-out of the facility, as well as subsequent tenant buildouts.
