NCSS delivered a custom fabricated architectural metal chain ceiling feature at Dolly Parton's new Life of Many Colors Museum that opened its doors to fans from across the world in October 2026.

Forty-two tracks were created in a variation of patterns to give the ceiling above her Fan Art Display a continuous flowing effect. Seven unique colors were specifically chosen to match the paint colors in the display cases below.

Detailed coordination with surrounding fire protection, HVAC, and lighting systems was required for this long-lead time item to ensure the entire system fit without conflict.

Dolly was intimately involved with so many parts of this project, with a vast number of personal keepsakes donated and displayed within the museum, the entire building is an emotional celebration of her life.
